Is Your Farm Website Working?

A 10-minute self-check. Pull up your website on your phone, then Google your farm's name. Answer yes or no, then count your yes answers.

YesNo

Getting found

  1. When you Google your farm's name, does your Google listing show up with the right hours, address or pickup spot, and phone number?
  2. Does your Google listing have photos from the past year?
  3. Does your website show up on the first page when you Google your farm's name?
  4. Do your website and your Google listing list the same hours?

On a phone

  1. Does your site load in a few seconds on your phone?
  2. Can you read it without pinching or zooming?
  3. Without scrolling much, can a stranger tell what you sell and where to get it?
  4. Can someone tap your phone number or email to reach you?

Buying from you

  1. Can a customer order or reserve online, or at least see clearly how to buy?
  2. Are prices, or a price range, on the site?
  3. Are pickup days, pickup spots, and delivery area spelled out?
  4. Are sold-out or out-of-season items marked?

Keeping in touch

  1. Is there a way to join your email list on your website?
  2. Have you emailed your customers in the last 30 days?
  3. Have you updated anything on your site in the last 3 months?

Your score: yes out of 15

13–15 yesYou're in good shape. Fix the few no's and keep it up. 8–12 yesA few things are getting in the way. Start with the section where you had the most no's. 0–7 yesIt may be time for a fresh start. A refresh is usually less work than patching an old site.
